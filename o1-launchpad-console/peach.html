Two simplifications vs. the real product UI: (1) logo is sent as a plain URL/URI, skipping peach.ag's own signed IPFS-upload session (their backend, not on-chain); (2) when doing a creator initial buy, minInitialTokenOut is sent as 0 (no slippage floor) rather than replicating their bonding-curve quote math — acceptable since this is testnet-only and a revert only costs test gas.
